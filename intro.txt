Design patterns are common architectural approaches in OOP

Gamma categorization splits design patterns into three categoreis
Creational patterns:
- creation/constructuon of an object
- explicit vs implicit(DI, reflection)
- wholesale(single statement) vs piecewise(step by step)

Structural Patterns:
- Concerned with structure
- May patterns are wrappers that mimic underlying class' interface
- Stress the importance of good API design

Behavioural Patterns:
- Different, no central theme with some overlap and solve unique problems
